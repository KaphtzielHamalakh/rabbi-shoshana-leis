A human being is the space between us.

Rabbi Sharon Brous · October 10, 2026

It’s Time to Write a New Beginning

We who believe in the dignity of every person, created in the image of God, will never adjust comfortably to a reality or a movement or a norm that rests on domination, subjugation, dehumanization.

…Not in international politics,
not in movements for social change,
not in personal relationships.

“I think if you’re not angry there’s something wrong,” said one Cornell student.

That student is right. The anger is not misplaced.

But the irrepressible sense of misalignment could be the birthplace of cynicism, rage, and resentment. Or it could ignite within us the kind of vitality, of cognitive and spiritual dissonance that demands that we birth a new story.

And of course there is another story to tell here, one already embedded in the text itself in Genesis I.

On this Shabbat Bereishit, I hear the invitation to a new narrative, one that challenges– even overturns– the dominant assumptions of its time.

The kind of hutzpah that says: this way is not the only way.

— Rabbi Sharon Brous, IKAR
